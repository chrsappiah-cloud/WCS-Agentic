## Purpose

The Board Charter defines the role and responsibilities of the Board, the relationship between the Board and management, and the expected standard of governance.

## Core Responsibilities

The Board approves strategy, budgets, risk appetite, major transactions, capital decisions, policies and executive oversight.

## Composition

The Board should include founder, investor and independent skills as the company scales. The Chair leads the Board and the CEO manages day-to-day operations within delegated limits.

## Meetings and Information Flow

The Board meets at least quarterly, receives papers in advance, maintains minutes and tracks action registers.

## Conduct and Conflicts

Directors act in the best interests of the company, disclose conflicts and comply with statutory duties.
