## Purpose

Retain information only as long as necessary for legal, regulatory, contractual and business reasons, then securely dispose of or anonymise it.

## Retention Schedule

The schedule covers customer records, student data, coursework, logs, analytics, financial records and HR records. Australian financial and tax records are commonly retained for at least seven years.

## Legal Holds

Deletion pauses where litigation, investigation, audit or other lawful hold applies.

## Deletion Requests

Deletion requests are handled through christopher.appiahthompson@myworldclass.org or chrsappiah@gmail.com, subject to lawful retention overrides.

## Evidence

Deletion and anonymisation should be documented and auditable where technically possible.

This draft must be reviewed by the named owner and approver before production use. Legal, tax, privacy, security and accounting content must be reviewed by qualified Australian advisers where applicable.
